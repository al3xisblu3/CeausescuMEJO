The Aftermath

Ceaușescu’s fall ended his rule, but it did not settle Romania’s future or resolve the losses of December 1989.

The human cost

More than 1,100 people died during the revolution. Much of the bloodshed occurred after Ceaușescu fled. Remembering those deaths is essential to understanding why the events remain contested.

A difficult transition

Ion Iliescu became a central figure in post-communist Romania. The country moved toward a market economy and later joined NATO and the European Union. Disagreement over Iliescu’s role and the violence of the transition remained part of public life.
