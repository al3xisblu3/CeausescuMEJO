The Execution

AI disclosure: The article text on this page was generated with assistance from OpenAI ChatGPT for MEJO 187 Project 2.

On Christmas Day 1989, the revolution reached its most final act: the execution of Nicolae and Elena Ceaușescu.

A rapid end

After fleeing Bucharest, the couple were captured. On December 25, a military tribunal tried them, and their death sentences were carried out that day.

Reading the record

The speed of the proceedings matters when studying this event. A hurried trial during a violent transfer of power offered little opportunity for a full public examination of the regime’s actions.
